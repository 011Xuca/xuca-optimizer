# Opciones de Debloat, Gaming y Limpieza, por su Id (el mismo Id que en Debloat.ps1, Gaming.ps1 y XucaOP.ps1).
# Cada bloque empieza con [Pagina Id] y lleva Titulo, Hace, Favor y Contra.
# Si cambias el texto en espanol de una opcion, esto sigue andando: solo acordate de actualizar el ingles.
# Si a una opcion le falta su bloque (o un campo), se muestra en espanol.

# ============================================================
# DEBLOAT
# ============================================================

[Debloat rpo]
Titulo: Turn off restore points for good
Hace: turns off restore points for good and deletes the ones you already have.
Favor: frees disk space and Windows stops writing copies in the background.
Contra: if something goes wrong you won't be able to go back with a restore point.

[Debloat av]
Titulo: Turn off ALL the antivirus
Hace: turns off Defender, SmartScreen and Smart App Control.
Favor: less CPU and disk use, and no scans in the middle of a match.
Contra: the PC is left without virus protection and Smart App Control can't be turned back on without reinstalling Windows.

[Debloat bl]
Titulo: Disable BitLocker
Hace: decrypts the disk and stops Windows from encrypting it again on its own.
Favor: a bit more disk speed and it won't lock you out if you change hardware or the BIOS.
Contra: if your PC is stolen, they can read your files.

[Debloat hib]
Titulo: Disable hibernation
Hace: turns off hibernation and Fast Startup, and turns on memory compression.
Favor: frees several GB of disk and every shutdown is complete, with fewer weird errors.
Contra: turning the PC on from off takes a few more seconds.

[Debloat mc]
Titulo: Turn on memory compression
Hace: turns on memory compression: Windows compresses what is in RAM and not being used, instead of sending it to disk.
Favor: with 4 to 8 GB of RAM more programs fit open and there is less stutter from using the page file.
Contra: uses a bit of CPU to compress. With 16 GB or more it barely changes anything. It needs the SysMain service turned on.

[Debloat ofb]
Titulo: Turn off Office Startup Boost
Hace: turns off the "Office Startup Boost" tasks that preload Word, Excel and PowerPoint when you sign in. If you don't have Office, nothing changes.
Favor: the RAM Office was using in the background is freed (tens to hundreds of MB, depending on the version).
Contra: the first time you open Word, Excel or PowerPoint it takes a bit longer. A big Office update can turn it back on.

[Debloat rc]
Titulo: Disable Recall
Hace: turns off Recall, which takes screenshots of what you do to search it with AI.
Favor: more privacy and less disk and CPU use.
Contra: you lose that screenshot search; it only exists on Copilot+ PCs.

[Debloat sch]
Titulo: Mute Windows sounds
Hace: mutes Windows sounds and the startup sound; only the device connect and disconnect sounds remain.
Favor: no alert sounds in the middle of a match or a stream.
Contra: you don't hear system alerts.

[Debloat fx]
Titulo: Reduce visual effects
Hace: removes animations, shadows, fades and transparency.
Favor: Windows feels faster and uses the graphics card a bit less.
Contra: Windows looks plainer.

[Debloat et]
Titulo: Add End task to the taskbar right-click menu
Hace: adds End task to the taskbar right-click menu.
Favor: you close a frozen game or program without opening Task Manager.
Contra: none.

[Debloat cal]
Titulo: Remove the calendar from the clock
Hace: the date and time on the taskbar are still shown, but clicking them no longer opens the calendar or the notification center.
Favor: the panel doesn't open by accident in the middle of a match and the taskbar stays the same.
Contra: you don't see the calendar or the notification history; Win + N doesn't open it either.

[Debloat apps]
Titulo: Block Store apps in the background
Hace: stops Store apps from running when you aren't using them. The Xbox app, the Game Bar, Gaming Services and Minecraft can still run in the background.
Favor: saves RAM and CPU.
Contra: some apps don't notify you of anything until you open them.

[Debloat ini]
Titulo: Turn off junk startup apps
Hace: turns off the programs that open by themselves when the PC starts (Discord, Spotify, Steam, Epic, Teams, OneDrive, Edge and similar). It doesn't touch Windows stuff, drivers, mouse, keyboard, headset and RGB software (Logitech, Razer, SteelSeries Sonar, Corsair...), Wallpaper Engine, antivirus, VPN or anti-cheats.
Favor: the PC boots faster and more RAM stays free.
Contra: those programs no longer open by themselves: you open them when you need them.

[Debloat tel]
Titulo: Reduce telemetry and get the newest Windows updates for lower RAM use
Hace: turns off the service that sends data to Microsoft and its tasks (disk and power diagnostics, error queue, MAR backup, startup measurement and maps), and sets the level Windows asks for to get new versions sooner.
Favor: fewer background processes using disk and CPU, and you get 26H2 first, which uses less RAM.
Contra: it's not a total shutdown of data: it's the middle ground to keep getting new things. Task Manager stops measuring the startup impact of programs.

[Debloat wg]
Titulo: Disable Widgets
Hace: removes the Widgets panel and hides the Task View button.
Favor: fewer processes and less RAM, and a cleaner taskbar.
Contra: you lose the weather and news panel.

[Debloat do]
Titulo: Stop sharing updates with other PCs
Hace: your PC stops sending updates to other PCs over the internet.
Favor: it doesn't use your upload bandwidth and there's less risk of lag in matches.
Contra: none for you.

[Debloat wud]
Titulo: Stop Windows Update from installing drivers
Hace: Windows Update stops installing drivers.
Favor: it won't change the graphics driver you chose or install old drivers.
Contra: you update drivers yourself, from the NVIDIA, AMD or manufacturer app.

[Debloat wup]
Titulo: Allow pausing Windows updates for a long time
Hace: lets you choose a pause of many years in Windows Update.
Favor: it won't update or restart during a tournament or an important match.
Contra: if you leave it paused for a long time, you miss security patches.

[Debloat ad]
Titulo: Remove ads, suggestions and Bing
Hace: removes ads, the advertising ID, suggested apps and the ones Microsoft and the manufacturer install on their own, Start recommendations and Bing from search.
Favor: a cleaner Start and search only looks in your PC, faster.
Contra: Start search no longer searches the internet.

[Debloat sv]
Titulo: Disable services and features almost nobody uses
Hace: turns off Fax, Maps, demo mode, geolocation and error report services, and removes Internet Explorer, Fax and Scan and the old Media Player.
Favor: fewer services and features loaded in Windows.
Contra: if you use the Windows scanner or the old Media Player, they're gone.

[Debloat rsv]
Titulo: Turn off more services to free RAM
Hace: turns off the Phone, Wallet, NFC payments, mobile hotspot, parental controls, smart cards, Insider and shortcut tracking services. It doesn't touch the Xbox services or Gaming Services.
Favor: fewer services loaded: frees some RAM and background processes.
Contra: the mobile hotspot, smart card readers and Microsoft parental controls stop working.

[Debloat xbs]
Titulo: Turn off the Xbox services
Hace: turns off the Xbox Live services: sign-in, cloud saves, Xbox networking and Xbox accessories. It doesn't touch Gaming Services.
Favor: a few MB less: these services are almost always stopped if you don't use Xbox.
Contra: Game Pass, Minecraft (Bedrock and the Launcher), Store games with cloud saves, Xbox chat and Xbox controller settings stop working until you reset it.

[Debloat dia]
Titulo: Set the diagnostic services to manual
Hace: sets the Diagnostic Policy Service (DPS) to manual; it also starts the diagnostic hosts and the resource usage monitor.
Favor: one to three fewer background processes: a few tens of MB of RAM (estimated, not measured).
Contra: Windows stops detecting problems on its own, and app history, battery usage per app and data usage per app in Settings stop updating. If a troubleshooter says the diagnostic service isn't running, reset this option.

[Debloat svh]
Titulo: Merge Windows services into fewer processes
Hace: makes Windows group its services into a few svchost processes instead of one per service (SvcHostSplitThresholdInKB).
Favor: saves several hundred MB of RAM and lowers the process count.
Contra: if one service hangs it can take down the ones sharing its process. Applies on restart.

[Debloat pv]
Titulo: Turn off tracking and data collection
Hace: turns off activity history, location, Wifi Sense, cloud clipboard and settings, typing learning and suggestions, cloud speech and apps listening for voice commands, maps, DRM and Homegroup.
Favor: more privacy and fewer background tasks.
Contra: apps that use location, like weather, can't detect it, and your settings aren't copied to other PCs.

[Debloat ter]
Titulo: Disable Teredo
Hace: turns off Teredo, an IPv6 tunnel Windows uses on top of IPv4.
Favor: it can remove extra latency on some connections.
Contra: it can affect Xbox party chat and NAT in Xbox games on PC.

[Debloat tp]
Titulo: Turn off program telemetry
Hace: turns off telemetry for Office, NVIDIA and PowerShell 7.
Favor: less data sent and fewer background tasks.
Contra: none in normal use.

[Debloat hst]
Titulo: Block Microsoft telemetry servers
Hace: adds 38 telemetry servers to the hosts file so they don't respond.
Favor: cuts data sending even if Windows turns something back on later.
Contra: Defender sometimes warns about hosts changes and some Microsoft diagnostics may fail.

[Debloat eb]
Titulo: Reduce Edge and Brave bloat
Hace: turns off the sidebar, shopping, rewards, wallet, VPN and AI in Edge and Brave; in Edge it also removes Copilot (it doesn't read your pages or your history), AI writing, AI themes, DevTools AI and downloading local AI models.
Favor: lighter browsers without ads.
Contra: you lose those features if you used them.

[Debloat su]
Titulo: Stop automatic updates of Store apps
Hace: Store apps only update when you ask for it.
Favor: they don't download anything in the middle of a match.
Contra: you have to update them manually now and then. It also stops updates for Game Pass games, Minecraft and Gaming Services: some games won't let you play online until you update them from the Xbox app or the Store.

[Debloat ia]
Titulo: Turn off Windows AI features
Hace: turns off Click to Do, the AI in Notepad and Paint (image creator, Cocreator, generative fill and erase, remove background), the AI agent in Settings, Copilot in search, app access to generative AI and Copilot in Word and Excel.
Favor: fewer AI processes and more privacy.
Contra: you lose those features.

[Debloat cam]
Titulo: Allow multiple apps to use the camera
Hace: turns on "Allow multiple apps to use camera at the same time" (Frame Server).
Favor: you can use the camera in OBS, Discord, Teams, etc. at once without one blocking the other.
Contra: some camera controls can only be changed from the camera settings page.

[Debloat db]
Titulo: Delete bloatware apps
Hace: deletes OneDrive, Copilot, the AI packages and the apps that come preinstalled.
Favor: more space, less RAM and fewer processes.
Contra: it can't be reset: if you want one back, reinstall it from the Store. Your OneDrive files on the PC are not deleted.

[Debloat edge]
Titulo: Remove Edge, Bing and Get Started
Hace: uninstalls Edge, Bing and Get Started, but keeps WebView2, which other programs use.
Favor: fewer background processes and Edge stops opening by itself.
Contra: a big Windows update can bring it back.

[Debloat chr]
Titulo: Install Google Chrome
Hace: installs Google Chrome with winget.
Favor: you have a browser even if you removed Edge.
Contra: none.

# ============================================================
# GAMING
# ============================================================

[Gaming ms]
Titulo: Competitive mouse
Hace: removes mouse acceleration and the pointer trail, and sets the speed to the middle.
Favor: the same hand movement always moves the same: better aim.
Contra: on the desktop the mouse may feel different until you get used to it.

[Gaming tk]
Titulo: Disable accessibility shortcuts
Hace: turns off the accessibility shortcuts that turn on by accident while gaming: Shift 5 times, right Shift for 8 seconds, Num Lock for 5 seconds, Alt+Shift+Num Lock (mouse keys), Alt+Shift+Print Screen (high contrast) and Win+Ctrl+Enter (Narrator).
Favor: it doesn't kick you out of the game mid-match or change the colors of the whole screen.
Contra: if you use any of these features for accessibility, turn it on manually from Settings.

[Gaming du]
Titulo: Stop Windows from lowering the volume during calls
Hace: Windows stops lowering the game volume when there's a call, for example on Discord.
Favor: the game always sounds the same.
Contra: none.

[Gaming mm]
Titulo: Give games multimedia priority
Hace: the Windows multimedia scheduler gives games more priority. The network limit stays at 10, the Windows value, because removing it can make latency worse.
Favor: a bit more CPU for the game and less stutter.
Contra: the effect is small.

[Gaming pri]
Titulo: Give your games high priority
Hace: every time you open those games, Windows gives them more CPU time than what runs in the background.
Favor: less stutter when you have things open in the background, like Discord, the browser or OBS.
Contra: if the CPU reaches 100 percent, background stuff may stutter a bit (for example, the OBS stream).

[Gaming afi]
Titulo: Use CPU affinity for your games
Hace: a small background program puts your games on the best cores and moves Discord, Spotify and the browser to others.
Favor: background stuff doesn't steal cores from the game: steadier frametimes and less stutter, especially in Minecraft.
Contra: while you play, background stuff runs a bit slower.

[Gaming pw]
Titulo: Use the High performance plan
Hace: turns on the High performance power plan.
Favor: the CPU responds faster and doesn't lower its frequency as much.
Contra: more power use and heat, especially on laptops.

[Gaming nb]
Titulo: Prioritize the laptop battery
Hace: only when the laptop is on battery: turbo is used only when needed, the processor favors saving power, it slows down before spinning up the fans, and Windows battery saver turns on by itself at 30 percent. Plugged in, nothing changes.
Favor: the battery lasts quite a bit longer and the laptop runs cooler and quieter off the charger.
Contra: on battery it performs a bit less; plug it in to play.

[Gaming cpu]
Titulo: Turn on the processor Nitro Mode
Hace: keeps all cores active, turbo at maximum and in aggressive mode, fast speed ramp-up and fans before slowing down, with the PC plugged in; also Windows stops throttling background programs.
Favor: less stutter when the game suddenly asks for more CPU.
Contra: more power use, heat and fan noise.

[Gaming ll]
Titulo: Turn on Low latency
Hace: turns on Low latency (Low Latency Profile), a hidden Windows 11 feature that can also be turned on with ViVeTool and ID 58989092: when you open Start, menus, search or apps, the processor goes to maximum for 1 to 3 seconds.
Favor: Start, menus and apps open faster, especially on low-end PCs.
Contra: short spikes of power use and heat; Microsoft is still testing it and on powerful PCs it's barely noticeable.

[Gaming idle]
Titulo: Disable processor idle states (C-states)
Hace: turns on the hidden "Processor idle disable" setting of the active power plan, plugged in and on battery: the cores stop going to sleep (C-states) when they have no work. It saves your current values first so you can go back.
Favor: the processor doesn't take time to wake up: a bit less latency and fewer micro-stutters in competitive games.
Contra: the processor never rests: much more power use and heat even when you're not gaming, fans get louder, some processors lower their turbo because of temperature, and on laptops the battery lasts much less.

[Gaming gm]
Titulo: Turn on Game Mode
Hace: turns on Game Mode.
Favor: Windows prioritizes the game and holds back updates while you play.
Contra: nothing important.

[Gaming xb]
Titulo: Delete the Xbox Game Bar app
Hace: deletes the Xbox Game Bar app and its old package (also for new accounts and after Windows updates), turns off its recordings and stops the controller's Xbox button from opening it. On two-CCD Ryzen X3D (7900X3D, 7950X3D, 9900X3D, 9950X3D) it only turns off the recordings and the controller shortcut: there the app is needed.
Favor: fewer processes and nothing recording in the background.
Contra: you lose Windows captures and clips (you can use NVIDIA, AMD or OBS), Xbox chat and widgets in game, and the Win + G shortcut.

[Gaming notif]
Titulo: Disable Windows notifications
Hace: turns off pop-up notifications and the Notification Center.
Favor: nothing covers your screen in a match.
Contra: you don't see notifications from apps like Discord or email, or Xbox invites to games or parties. It doesn't help if an app comes to the front on its own (an update or a window from Discord, Brave or Spotify): that isn't a notification and can still happen.

[Gaming xm]
Titulo: Turn off Xbox mode
Hace: turns off Windows 11 Xbox mode (the console-style full screen with the Xbox app).
Favor: Windows won't open it or offer it if you don't use it. It doesn't change FPS: it's just for comfort.
Contra: to use the PC as a console with a controller, you have to turn it back on.

[Gaming xbt]
Titulo: Remove Xbox completely
Hace: turns off and disables the Xbox and Gaming Services services, deletes and deprovisions the Xbox app, Gaming Services, Xbox Identity Provider, Xbox TCUI, Xbox chat transcription and the Xbox Game Bar (also its old package), and turns off recordings (GameDVR), the controller shortcut, Xbox mode and the cloud save task. On two-CCD Ryzen X3D it keeps the Game Bar installed (the V-Cache driver uses it) and only turns off its recordings.
Favor: the most you can do to get rid of Xbox: no Xbox service, app or task stays loaded or starts with Windows.
Contra: Game Pass, Minecraft Bedrock and the Minecraft Launcher, and Microsoft Store and Xbox app games stop working, and you can't play online with your Xbox account. Reset reinstalls the Xbox app, Gaming Services, Xbox Identity Provider and the Game Bar with winget (needs internet); the rest is installed by the Xbox app or the Store. It asks for a restart.

[Gaming gf]
Titulo: Optimize windowed games and hardware GPU scheduling
Hace: uses the modern mode for windowed games and turns on hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling.
Favor: less latency in windowed and borderless, and it's needed for Frame Generation.
Contra: it can cause problems on some old PCs.

[Gaming fso]
Titulo: Disable fullscreen optimizations and DPI scaling on your games
Hace: for each installed game it ticks "Disable fullscreen optimizations" and "Override high DPI scaling behavior: Application" (Properties > Compatibility).
Favor: less input latency in fullscreen, and the game handles its own scaling without Windows stretching it.
Contra: it only touches games detected automatically (Steam, Epic, EA, etc.); if a game loses modern borderless mode, R undoes it. Close and reopen the game.

[Gaming mpo]
Titulo: Disable MPO
Hace: disables MPO, a display feature that overlays images.
Favor: fixes flickering, black screens and invisible cursor on some PCs.
Contra: if you didn't have those problems, GPU use in windowed mode may go up a bit.

[Gaming msi]
Titulo: Turn on MSI mode on the graphics and network cards
Hace: the cards notify the processor with messages (MSI) instead of using the old shared line.
Favor: a bit less latency and micro-stutter. Many new cards already come like this, in that case nothing changes.
Contra: if the driver doesn't support it well, after restarting you may see a black screen or lose internet.

[Gaming pca]
Titulo: Disable the Compatibility Assistant
Hace: turns off the Program Compatibility Assistant.
Favor: avoids windows that steal focus during a match.
Contra: it doesn't warn you about problems with old programs.

[Gaming tr]
Titulo: Lock the system timer at the minimum
Hace: keeps the Windows timer fixed at 0.5 ms for the whole system.
Favor: less latency and steadier frame times.
Contra: more power use and heat, especially on laptops.

[Gaming ram]
Titulo: Turn on automatic standby RAM cleaning
Hace: empties standby memory when less than 1024 MB are free.
Favor: avoids stutter when RAM fills up.
Contra: what was cached is read from disk again.

[Gaming sm]
Titulo: Disable SysMain and Prefetch
Hace: turns off SysMain and Prefetch, which preload programs into RAM.
Favor: leaves more RAM free for the game.
Contra: some programs open a bit slower the first time.

[Gaming pe]
Titulo: Disable USB, PCIe and disk power saving
Hace: turns off power saving for USB, USB 3 and PCI Express, and with the PC plugged in the disks no longer turn off by themselves.
Favor: avoids mouse and keyboard micro-drops, and the few-second stutters when an HDD spins back up.
Contra: at idle the PC uses a few more watts and the graphics card and NVMe SSDs can run a bit hotter; on laptops the battery lasts less (also with the lid closed) and HDDs keep spinning while the PC is on.

[Gaming nic]
Titulo: Disable network power saving
Hace: turns off power saving on the network card.
Favor: fewer ping spikes.
Contra: a bit more power use; on bad cards it can cause drops.

[Gaming dup]
Titulo: Limit the cable to 1 Gbps
Hace: sets the wired card to a fixed 1 Gbps instead of automatic (Speed & Duplex = 1.0 Gbps Full Duplex). Only on 2.5 Gbps or faster cards, like Realtek 2.5GbE.
Favor: fixes the few-second drops where the cable disconnects and reconnects by itself. With internet of 1 Gbps or less you lose nothing.
Contra: the card won't go over 1 Gbps: if your internet is faster than 1 Gbps, or you copy files between PCs at home, it's slower.

[Gaming wf]
Titulo: Use WiFi and Ethernet together without drops
Hace: keeps WiFi and Ethernet connected at the same time: Windows no longer cuts WiFi when you plug in the cable, the cable goes first and WiFi isn't turned off to save power.
Favor: WiFi stops disconnecting by itself on PCs that have WiFi and Ethernet (like motherboards with built-in WiFi).
Contra: slightly more power use, because both connections stay on.

[Gaming nag]
Titulo: Disable Nagle's algorithm
Hace: turns off Nagle's algorithm on your network connections: Windows sends each small packet right away instead of bundling them and waiting.
Favor: it can lower ping by a few milliseconds in games that use TCP, like Minecraft or MMO and strategy games.
Contra: most modern shooters use UDP and notice no change; on slow connections network use may go up a bit.

[Gaming dns]
Titulo: Switch DNS to the fastest one for your connection
Hace: measures Cloudflare, Google, Quad9 and OpenDNS from your connection and sets the fastest.
Favor: logins, matchmaking and web pages load faster.
Contra: it doesn't lower in-game ping.

[Gaming mi]
Titulo: Disable Memory integrity
Hace: turns off Windows Memory integrity.
Favor: more FPS in some games.
Contra: less protection against malicious drivers.

[Gaming avl]
Titulo: Make the antivirus use less CPU
Hace: limits Defender scans to 5 percent of CPU, with low priority.
Favor: scans don't lower your FPS.
Contra: scans take longer to finish.

[Gaming vbs]
Titulo: Disable VBS
Hace: turns off virtualization-based security.
Favor: between 5 and 10 percent more FPS.
Contra: WSL, Docker, Hyper-V and virtual machines stop working.

[Gaming spc]
Titulo: Disable Spectre and Meltdown protections
Hace: turns off Windows' protections against Spectre (variant 2) and Meltdown, two processor flaws, using Microsoft's official method (FeatureSettingsOverride = 3 and FeatureSettingsOverrideMask = 3). Only available if your processor has those protections turned on.
Favor: on older processors (Intel 8th gen or earlier) it can give a few more FPS and less stutter in games that use the processor or the disk a lot. On newer processors the difference is usually almost zero.
Contra: your PC is left exposed: a malicious web page or program could read data from memory, like passwords. Don't use it on a PC with important data or one shared by several people. Some anti-cheats or work programs may complain.

[Gaming wsr]
Titulo: Disable Windows Search
Hace: turns off file indexing.
Favor: frees disk and CPU.
Contra: Start search is slower.

[Gaming stg]
Titulo: Disable Storage Sense
Hace: Windows stops deleting temporary files on its own.
Favor: it doesn't delete anything by itself while you play.
Contra: you do the cleaning yourself, for example with the app's Clean cache.

[Gaming ext]
Titulo: Show file extensions and hidden items
Hace: shows the end of file names, like .exe or .txt, and hidden files and folders.
Favor: you see what type of file it is, spot disguised viruses and find folders like AppData.
Contra: you see hidden system files: don't delete them if you don't know what they are.

[Gaming vfx]
Titulo: Set Windows visual effects to performance
Hace: sets Windows visual effects to performance mode: fewer animations, shadows and transparency.
Favor: frees a bit of GPU and CPU on low-power PCs, especially if the game is borderline.
Contra: Windows looks plainer. On a powerful PC it barely changes FPS; Restore undoes it.

[Gaming md]
Titulo: Remove the menu delay
Hace: lowers the wait when opening menus from 400 to 0 ms.
Favor: Windows feels faster.
Contra: none.

[Gaming v4]
Titulo: Prefer IPv4 over IPv6
Hace: Windows uses IPv4 before IPv6 when both are available.
Favor: it can lower latency with some servers.
Contra: almost none; IPv6 keeps working.

# ============================================================
# LIMPIEZA
# ============================================================

[Limpieza CLEAN_BIN]
Titulo: Empty the Recycle Bin
Hace: empties the Recycle Bin on all disks.
Favor: frees the space of what you already deleted.
Contra: what's in the Recycle Bin can't be recovered anymore.

[Limpieza CLEAN_GPU]
Titulo: Delete the shader cache
Hace: deletes the shaders that games already compiled for your graphics card.
Favor: fixes stutter or weird graphics after updating the graphics driver.
Contra: the first match of each game will stutter while it rebuilds them.

[Limpieza CLEAN_RP]
Titulo: Delete restore points
Hace: deletes saved restore points; system protection stays on.
Favor: frees several GB of disk.
Contra: if something goes wrong, you can't go back to how Windows was before.

[Debloat rmin]
Titulo: Turn off printing, network sharing and Remote Desktop
Hace: turns off or sets to manual the printing services, the ones that make your PC show up on the local network and cast to the TV, the web folder service (WebClient), media sharing and work folders, and Remote Desktop.
Favor: fewer programs running in the background and a bit of RAM freed.
Contra: you can't print or save to PDF from Print, your PC doesn't show up on the local network, you can't cast to the TV and nobody can get in through Remote Desktop, until you restore it.

[Debloat nvl]
Titulo: Turn off the extra NVIDIA services and tasks
Hace: sets to manual and stops the service used by the NVIDIA app, the overlay and ShadowPlay recording, and turns off the one that sends usage data plus the tasks that look for drivers and updates. It doesn't touch the display service: without it the NVIDIA Control Panel doesn't open and colors and G-Sync aren't kept.
Favor: fewer NVIDIA processes open and a bit of RAM freed, without losing the driver.
Contra: the overlay, ShadowPlay and the NVIDIA app filters don't work until the service runs. If the app doesn't open well, restore the option. You update the drivers yourself.

[Debloat nvt]
Titulo: Remove the NVIDIA icon from the system tray
Hace: stops the NVIDIA tray icon (NvTray) from opening by itself at sign-in. The driver, Control Panel and NVIDIA app stay installed and open from the Start menu. You'll notice it after a restart.
Favor: the annoying tray icon goes away and the RAM its process uses is freed.
Contra: you lose the quick access from the tray; open the app or Control Panel from Start or the desktop.

[Debloat srh]
Titulo: Remove the search box from the taskbar
Hace: hides the taskbar search box and turns off search highlights. Without the box, Windows doesn't keep the SearchHost process and its several WebView2 processes open. You still search with the Windows key.
Favor: the "Search" group with its 5 to 7 processes goes away and RAM is freed.
Contra: you don't see the search box on the taskbar; to search, press the Windows key and type.

[Debloat upd]
Titulo: Set the Edge, Chrome and Brave updaters to manual
Hace: makes the programs that update Edge, Chrome and Brave stop running all the time: Windows starts them only when needed.
Favor: fewer processes open all the time and a bit of RAM freed.
Contra: none you'd notice: the browsers still update on their own. If you don't have one of those browsers, it does nothing for it.

[Debloat tsk]
Titulo: Turn off background maintenance and diagnostic tasks
Hace: turns off automatic Windows tasks that wake up the processor and disk without you noticing: program and device inventory, Family Safety, media sharing, Maps, network diagnostics and sending disk and system data.
Favor: fewer processor and disk spikes when you're not doing anything.
Contra: Windows stops doing that automatic maintenance. It doesn't touch Windows Update, restore points or SSD optimization.

[Debloat cdp]
Titulo: Turn off cross-device sync and clipboard history
Hace: turns off the Connected Devices Platform (shared experiences and Nearby Sharing) and the clipboard history and sync.
Favor: fewer sync processes and less data sent.
Contra: Win+V stops saving the clipboard history, and you can't send files or clipboard between your PC and your phone or other PCs.

[Debloat fsb]
Titulo: Stop the disk from noting when you open each file
Hace: makes Windows stop writing to the disk the date you opened each file.
Favor: less is written to the disk and a bit less background work, mostly on hard drives.
Contra: Explorer stops showing the real last-access date of files. Some old backup programs use it.

[Debloat rap]
Titulo: Speed up shutdown and desktop startup
Hace: lowers to 2 seconds how long Windows waits to close services and programs when shutting down, and removes the 10-second delay Windows puts on startup programs.
Favor: the PC turns off and on faster.
Contra: frozen services and programs are closed after 2 seconds; Windows still warns you if something is unsaved.

[Debloat sx]
Titulo: Turn off even more services almost nobody uses
Hace: turns off rarely used services: biometric sign-in (Windows Hello with fingerprint or face), Sensors, Wi-Fi Direct and Windows Connect Now, Telephony (TAPI), iSCSI, File History, Windows Backup, Remote events, the ActiveX installer, Enterprise App Management, guided troubleshooting, the RPC locator and more.
Favor: fewer services open and fewer Windows processes in the background.
Contra: Windows Hello (fingerprint or face), sensors (auto brightness or rotation), Wi-Fi Direct and File History stop working. If something fails, restore the option.

[Debloat bt]
Titulo: Turn off Bluetooth
Hace: turns off the Bluetooth support service, the Bluetooth audio one and the remote control profiles one (AVCTP). It won't apply if a Bluetooth device is connected right now.
Favor: fewer services loaded.
Contra: Bluetooth headphones, mouse, keyboard and controllers stop working until you restore it.

[Debloat str]
Titulo: Turn off the Microsoft Store install services
Hace: turns off the service that installs Store apps, the one that checks their licenses and the one that prepares them.
Favor: fewer services open and a bit of RAM freed.
Contra: you can't install or update Microsoft Store apps (nor Xbox or Game Pass games). Some Store apps may stop opening because they can't check their license.

[Debloat wus]
Titulo: Turn off Windows Update completely
Hace: turns off the Windows Update service and the update orchestrator, and blocks automatic updates by policy.
Favor: no background downloads or installs, no surprise restarts and less RAM, CPU and disk.
Contra: the PC gets no security or driver patches and is exposed to known flaws. Restore it every so often to update.

[Debloat thm]
Titulo: Show icons instead of thumbnails in Explorer
Hace: Explorer always shows icons, doesn't make thumbnails and doesn't keep the thumbnail cache.
Favor: Explorer uses less RAM and opens photo and video folders faster.
Contra: you don't see the preview of images or videos in folders.

[Debloat leg]
Titulo: Turn off legacy Windows features
Hace: turns off Remote Assistance, AutoPlay for USBs and discs, and Windows error reporting.
Favor: fewer services and features loaded, and an unknown USB no longer opens by itself.
Contra: nobody can connect to your PC with Remote Assistance, and when you plug in a USB the what-to-do menu doesn't show up.

[Debloat old]
Titulo: Remove all the old Windows components nobody uses
Hace: turns off Internet Explorer, Fax and Scan, the old Media Player, SMBv1 (the old, insecure network protocol), PowerShell 2.0, Telnet, TFTP, Work Folders, the XPS viewer and services, Remote Differential Compression, DirectPlay and the legacy components, and removes WordPad, Steps Recorder and the Math Recognizer.
Favor: fewer features loaded and less attack surface (SMBv1 and PowerShell 2 are classic targets), and frees disk space.
Contra: very old games that need DirectPlay don't work, and you can't share files with very old computers (SMBv1) or use Telnet. Going back needs internet.

[Debloat msx]
Titulo: Remove the Microsoft Store
Hace: uninstalls the Microsoft Store and the Store purchase app for all users and removes them from the apps installed on new accounts.
Favor: fewer processes, less RAM and nothing from the Store updating apps in the background.
Contra: you can't install or update Store apps (nor Game Pass, Xbox or Minecraft Bedrock). Getting it back needs internet (it's reinstalled with wsreset -i).

[Debloat usv]
Titulo: Turn off the contacts, calendar, messages and sync user services
Hace: turns off the services Windows starts for each user: contacts and calendar (UserDataSvc, Unistore, PimIndex), sync (OneSync), messages, Connected Devices, cloud clipboard, user printing and Web Defense.
Favor: fewer svchost processes per session and a bit less RAM.
Contra: the Mail, Calendar and People apps don't sync, the cloud clipboard and Nearby Sharing stop working, and Defender web protection doesn't run.

[Debloat dold]
Titulo: Delete the old Windows apps and keep only the modern ones
Hace: removes the classic versions Windows has always shipped, installed apart from the modern ones: the system Notepad (Windows has two), classic Paint, WordPad, classic Windows Media Player, Steps Recorder, PowerShell ISE, Internet Explorer, the XPS viewer and Fax and Scan. The modern apps (new Notepad and Photos, Paint, Calculator, Snipping Tool) stay, and if one is missing it's installed with winget (needs internet). Then it trims the space that's no longer used. It can't be ticked together with "Delete the modern apps".
Favor: you keep a single version of each app, the new one, and free space and loaded components.
Contra: if you also delete the modern ones, you're left without Notepad or Paint. Getting the old ones back needs internet (the restore button downloads them again).

[Debloat dball]
Titulo: Delete the modern Windows apps and keep only the old ones
Hace: uninstalls for all users and removes from new accounts every modern Windows app that can be deleted, except the Store, the app installer (winget), Terminal, Xbox and Gaming Services, and what Windows needs to work. If you're missing an old one (system Notepad, classic Paint, WordPad, classic Media Player) it installs it first, then trims the space that's no longer used. Calculator, screen capture (Win+Shift+S), video and image codecs and your manufacturer's audio and video panels stay. It's the opposite of "Delete the old apps" and can't be ticked together with it.
Favor: fewer processes, less RAM and a lot of free disk space.
Contra: IT CAN'T BE RESTORED: Photos, Notepad, Paint and the others go away. Some can be reinstalled by hand from the Store or with winget; if something breaks, you have to reinstall Windows.

[Debloat rbs]
Titulo: Delete the Windows update backups
Hace: runs DISM with ResetBase: deletes from the component store (WinSxS) the old versions of each component that updates left behind.
Favor: frees several GB of disk.
Contra: IT CAN'T BE RESTORED: you can no longer uninstall the updates you have installed or go back to an earlier version of a component. If an update breaks something, the only way out is reinstalling Windows. It takes several minutes.

[Debloat drs]
Titulo: Delete old drivers and drivers for hardware you no longer use
Hace: deletes from the driver store every third-party driver that no installed device is using: old driver versions and drivers for hardware you no longer have. Windows won't delete the ones in use.
Favor: frees 1 to several GB and removes old drivers that can cause conflicts.
Contra: IT CAN'T BE RESTORED: if you plug in an old printer, controller or other device again, you have to download its driver again (or Windows Update looks for it). The driver of the graphics card in use isn't touched. Don't use it together with Windows Update driver search turned off: a new device would be left without a driver.

[Debloat wre]
Titulo: Delete the Windows recovery environment
Hace: turns off the Windows Recovery Environment (WinRE) and deletes its image (Winre.wim).
Favor: frees about 500 MB to 1 GB of disk.
Contra: IT CAN'T BE RESTORED: if Windows doesn't start, it no longer has the repair menu or "Reset this PC": the only way out is reinstalling Windows from a USB drive.

[Debloat lang]
Titulo: Delete language packs you don't use
Hace: removes the language components (handwriting, text recognition, speech and read-aloud) of every language that isn't in your language list or English.
Favor: frees space and removes components you never use.
Contra: IT CAN'T BE RESTORED: if you later add another language, Windows downloads it again over the internet, but what was deleted doesn't come back by itself. Your language and English aren't touched.

[Debloat acc]
Titulo: Delete accessibility and speech aids
Hace: uninstalls from Windows the speech components (screen reader and dictation), handwriting recognition, the text-in-image reader (OCR) and Braille support, for every language including yours. It also turns off the Sticky Keys, Filter Keys and Toggle Keys pop-ups that appear when you press Shift 5 times.
Favor: frees space and removes components you don't use if you don't need those aids.
Contra: IT CAN'T BE RESTORED from the app: if you ever need them (Narrator, voice dictation, pen), you have to download them again from Settings > Accessibility / Language. Not for people who use these aids.

[Gaming dvr]
Titulo: Turn off background game recording
Hace: turns off the clip capture and recording of Xbox Game Bar (Game DVR), which records in the background while you play. It doesn't delete the Game Bar or touch Xbox.
Favor: more FPS and less stutter, mostly if background recording was on.
Contra: you can't record clips with Win+Alt+G or with the Game Bar.

[Gaming fgb]
Titulo: Give more processor time to the program you're using
Hace: changes how Windows splits processor time (Win32PrioritySeparation = 38): the window you're in, such as the game, gets longer turns and goes ahead of background programs.
Favor: the game responds more evenly when there are programs open in the background.
Contra: background programs (downloads, browser) run a bit slower while you play.

[Limpieza CLEAN_OLD]
Titulo: Delete the Windows.old folder
Hace: deletes C:\Windows.old, the copy of your previous Windows left after a big update. It takes 5 to 20 minutes.
Favor: frees 10 to 30 GB.
Contra: you can no longer go back to the previous version of Windows.

[Limpieza CLEAN_LAUNCH]
Titulo: Delete the Discord, Steam, Epic and Store cache
Hace: deletes only the web cache of Discord, Steam and Epic Games Launcher, and the local cache of the Microsoft Store. It doesn't touch your games, your saved games or your accounts.
Favor: frees space and fixes launchers that load badly.
Contra: the first time you open those apps they take a bit longer to load.

[Limpieza CLEAN_BRW]
Titulo: Delete the browsers cache
Hace: deletes only the cache of Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox (images and files of pages already visited). It doesn't touch passwords, cookies, history, bookmarks or signed-in sessions.
Favor: frees hundreds of MB to several GB and fixes pages that load badly.
Contra: pages take a bit longer to load the first time.

[Limpieza CLEAN_EVT]
Titulo: Clear the Event Viewer
Hace: deletes the history of Windows events and errors (all logs).
Favor: removes junk and old logs.
Contra: you lose the history used to find the cause of a blue screen or a weird error.

[Limpieza CLEAN_DISM]
Titulo: Clean up the old Windows components (DISM)
Hace: runs the normal cleanup of the component store (WinSxS): removes old versions of components that are no longer needed. You can still uninstall updates.
Favor: frees disk space.
Contra: it takes several minutes.

[Limpieza CLEAN_APPS]
Titulo: Delete the leftovers of uninstalled apps
Hace: looks for what uninstalled apps left behind: data of uninstalled Store apps, program entries without files (with their AppData and ProgramData folders and registry keys of the same name) and empty folders in Program Files.
Favor: frees space and removes registry junk.
Contra: a folder with saved games (Save, Saved, SaveGames) isn't touched, but be careful anyway: if you uninstalled a game that keeps its saves elsewhere, copy them first. What's deleted can't be recovered.

[Limpieza CLEAN_DL]
Titulo: Empty the Downloads folder
Hace: deletes EVERYTHING in each user's Downloads folder: installers, documents, photos and whatever you downloaded.
Favor: frees the space taken by old downloads.
Contra: IT DOESN'T GO TO THE RECYCLE BIN: what's there can't be recovered. Check the folder first.
